Published on November 17, 2026 · examples

The November 17 file itself stays untouched: it is history — its tasks have left its area to land where they keep living.

6. Updating the next day, and the day after

On Wednesday the 18th, repeat the same gesture:

bujo_new 2026 Nov 19
# fill in 2026-Nov-18-Wed during the day
bujo_migrate 2026-Nov-18-Wed

The cycle is the same every day. And on the last day of November:

bujo_new 2026 Dec
bujo_migrate 2026-Nov
migrated entries from 2026-Nov to 2026-Dec
migrated entries from 2026-Nov to 2026

The month's > lines slide into 2026-Dec, its < lines move up into the year 2026. At year's end, same logic with bujo_new 2027 then bujo_migrate 2026.

7. The daily routine, summarized

The daily gesture in three commands:
bujo_new 2026 Nov 19 — create tomorrow's day in advance
bujo_new 2026 Nov 20 — create the one after
bujo_migrate 2026-Nov-19-Thu — close the day

This rhythm — create, fill, migrate — is enough to keep the logbook alive for years, with no effort. Happy journaling!
