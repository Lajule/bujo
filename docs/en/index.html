Welcome to the logbook

Published on November 17, 2026 · where this logbook begins

Bujo is a small Bash script that organizes a daily log into year, month and day files. Inspired by the bullet journal method, it relies on nothing but plain text: one file per period, a handful of simple markers, and a single migration command to carry your tasks from one day to the next, one month to the next, one year to the next.

No app, no account: just your terminal, a script to load, and your files. This logbook explains how to use it day after day, with real example files.
